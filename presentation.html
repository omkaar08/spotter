Validation Framework
Out-Of-Time (OOT) Temporal Validation
⚠️ Why Random K-Fold Fails

Random cross-validation introduces severe temporal look-ahead leakage by using future loads to predict past loads in time-series data.

❌ Random K-Fold yields artificially optimistic metrics that fail in production deployment.
🛡️ Our OOT Split Solution
  • 1. Development Train: Jan 01 – Aug 31, 2025 (38,477 loads)
  • 2. Holdout Validation: Sept 01 – Oct 31, 2025 (9,523 loads)
  • 3. Target Encoding Guard: Out-of-fold computation without target exposure.
✓ Strictly mirrors official evaluation setup on Nov 01 – Dec 31, 2025 (`validation.csv`).
